PYQ BANK (subject-wise) — how it works
--------------------------------------
Open NotesVerse -> "PYQ Bank" button.
Polity is pre-loaded: 664 questions (UPSC CSE 423, CDS 172, CAPF 69), merged under common subtopics.
Click a subtopic -> every question of that subtopic from all three exams, with exam + year tags.
Per question: attempt (answer hidden until you pick), notes, flag, elimination technique, edit (pencil),
change subtopic (click the subtopic chip), Unattempt / Try again.
CDS and CAPF PDFs had no answers: filter "Answer key missing", click an option or "Show answer",
then press A/B/C/D under "Set the correct answer" — or use the "Answer key" button to paste many at once.
Your old PYQ tables (if any) are left untouched in the database.
